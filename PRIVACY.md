# Privacy Policy for no-short

Effective date: October 8, 2026

`no-short` is a package of reusable instruction-based skills. It does not operate a backend service, collect personal data, use cookies, or send user data to the developer.

The optional No Short Usage menu bar companion reads retained local Codex logs through the bundled usage engine. It refreshes every 15 seconds while running, including when Codex panels are closed, and stops its scans on quit or sleep. It shares the local snapshot cache and may fetch public model pricing from LiteLLM; no log contents, prompts or session titles are sent to the pricing provider. It adds no telemetry or developer-hosted backend. Source-folder preferences are saved in local UserDefaults. Opening at login is opt-in through macOS and does not install a separate daemon. Local session names and usage can be visible in the menu and its tooltips.

The skills may guide a user or agent to work with files, connected services, or websites that the user has chosen to use. Those services process data under their own terms and privacy policies. Review a skill and any connected-service permissions before use.

If you contact the project through a GitHub issue, the information you choose to include is processed by GitHub and may be visible according to that repository's settings. Do not include secrets or sensitive personal information in an issue.

For privacy questions or requests, open an issue at https://github.com/longlonglong173/no-short/issues.
