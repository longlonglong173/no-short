Know when context reaches your threshold

The ring around each logo shows roughly how much of the model’s context capacity the session is using.

A FEW DETAILS

Good to know.

Are these my actual subscription charges?+

No. Costs are API-equivalent estimates calculated from retained local logs and public model prices. They are not invoices, deducted credits or remaining subscription quota.

Do I need the Codex plugin?+

The menu bar app works independently. You can install the optional Codex plugin separately to see a usage panel in a compatible Desktop chat running on your Mac.
