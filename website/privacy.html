WEBSITE PRIVACY

Website privacy and data

This site introduces no-short and links to the project’s source and documentation. It does not ask you to create an account or read data from your AI agent.

Data on this website

The site includes no analytics integrations, advertising, or information collection forms. Your browser saves your language and light/dark preferences for your next visit. Fonts are served with the website.

Hosting

The website is hosted on Vercel. When you visit, Vercel may process connection information and request logs under its own policy.

Vercel’s privacy policy ↗

No Short Usage

The menu bar app processes logs on your Mac. If you also install the MCP plugin, the selected session’s title, ID and usage are returned to ChatGPT/Codex and may become input to the AI model. The full session list is sent along as metadata for the usage panel. Local caches persist until replaced or deleted. The project policy explains who receives data, retention and removal.

External links

GitHub links take you to a different service with its own policy. Installing and using no-short involves different data handling from simply visiting this website.
